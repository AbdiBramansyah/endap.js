parabolic motion: x−t, y−t, y−x

The x−t plot is linear because vx is constant, with slope v0x = 20. The y−t plot is quadratic because ay is constant; it peaks at t = 4 s (y = 80) and returns to y = 0 at t = 8 s. Eliminating t gives the trajectory y(x) = (v0y/v0x)x − (|ay|/(2v0x2))x2 = 2x − x2/80, which is the y−x plot.
